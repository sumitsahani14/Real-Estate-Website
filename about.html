Since 1994

We kept the roster small on purpose.

Halcyon Estates started as two brokers and a rented office above a bakery in the Old Mill District. Three decades later, it's still a small team — by choice, not by circumstance.

Why we stay small

Six brokers. No teams beneath them.

Every broker at Halcyon carries their own listings from first walkthrough to closing table. We turned down the standard growth model — junior agents fielding calls under a senior broker's name — because it moves the person you hired further from the transaction, not closer.

It means we take on fewer homes than a typical agency our age would. It also means the broker who tells you what your home is worth is the same one negotiating on your behalf six weeks later.

History

Three decades, four towns

1994

Founded in the Old Mill District

Two brokers, one listing at a time, working out of a single room above what's now the neighborhood bakery.

2003

Expanded into Willowmere and Seabrook

Added two brokers who'd grown up in those towns rather than opening a branch office cold.

2015

Standish Valley

Took on our first farmhouses and larger acreage listings, requiring a different kind of diligence than in-town homes.

2024

412th home closed

Still six brokers. Still one office. Still no listing marketed until a broker has walked it themselves.

What we hold ourselves to

Three working rules

Walk it first

No home goes on our site until a broker has walked it in person and can answer questions about it without checking notes.

Say no often

We decline more listings than we accept. If we wouldn't stand behind the asking price, we'll tell you before you list, not after it sits.

One point of contact

The broker you meet at the first showing is the one you'll be negotiating alongside at closing — never handed off midway.
