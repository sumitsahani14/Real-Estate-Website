How we work

One broker, start to close.

Most agencies hand a listing between departments — marketing, showings, negotiation, closing. At Halcyon, the broker who takes the listing is the one who answers your calls, negotiates the offer and sits across from you at closing. We keep our roster small enough that this stays true.

That also means we say no more than we say yes. We take on homes we would be glad to live in ourselves, in the four communities we know property by property.
